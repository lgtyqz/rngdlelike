# CSV catalog rules

The two CSV files in `catalog/` are the item source of truth. No old, unlisted items remain in the registries. Sheet text is preserved, including the `bullmaket` ID and identical Bear/Bull Market conditions.

The following defaults resolve details the sheets do not specify:

- A reel has a persistent pool of slots, initially 0–9. Permanent tools affect its currently landed pool slot, even if a temporary tool changed the displayed digit. Mirror clones the underlying slot. Pill increments its underlying numeric value, wrapping 9 to 0. Soap removes it and leaves a blank display until rerolled; it cannot remove the last pool entry. A bomb can remove the last entry, leaving that reel blank. The six reel positions remain in place.
- Bomb installation starts at 3. Its countdown scores as that number and is displayed over a bomb background. Each subsequent actual landing or a Decrement use lowers a real bomb's countdown; zero removes that pool entry and pays $100 once. Decrement can also target a copied bomb display, but that only changes the temporary display. Animations are cosmetic. Each displayed W doubles the earned goal bonus and appears in the scoring log after Get Points; it does not multiply the allowance or trinket cash.
- Superposition displays a trident until the player chooses 1 or 0, and Swap carries that choice state with the displayed reel. Temporary digit replacement ends that landing's choice. Lock fixes the selected reel at its current number for the next initial spin only. Neighbor tools wrap around the six reels.
- Limited uses last for the run. Upgrades add one remaining use and one capacity. ATM's extra use expires after the spin even on limited-use tools; temporary credit is consumed first. Cartwheeler gives five extra uses to Reroll One, Three, and All each spin.
- Points and most persistent trinket counters use the finalized scoring reels. Knife and Crystal Ball count matching numbers when they land on the initial spin and whenever a tool places or rerolls them, including repeat landings; scoring does not recount them. Camera adds one activation using the reels at the time it is used; points are included when the spin settles, while growth, cash, and immediate rewards happen immediately. Camera can activate entry/acquisition abilities (Bank and Package). Snail's Camera activation waits for the final goal result. Passive probability, discount, retrigger, and extra-use trinkets have no activated ability; Camera does not consume a use on them.
- Bean starts at 1 and grows by 5 per scored one. Seven and the Misers start at zero, grow from qualifying digits, then score their new accumulated power. Rabbit, Heaven, and Three score their existing power, then grow on divisibility. Zero is divisible by 3, 5, 7, and 11. Special non-digit symbols make the final number ineligible for divisibility.
- Eight Ball repeats contributions tied to an eight, including groups containing eights, and doubles growth/counting triggered by eights. It does not duplicate unconditional or whole-number conditions. Moai picks a favorite number on each spin and keeps it through tool use. Each final reel showing that number doubles the round score, with a separate scoring step on that reel. A favorite eight triggers twice per matching reel when Eight Ball is owned. All score multipliers apply after additive points, regardless of inventory order.
- Pairs count every matching pair; triples use disjoint groups. Mini-Flush and Flush mean at least four/five matches. Brainrot II requires an adjacent 6 then 7. Mini Straight counts each consecutive three-digit window; Straight counts each maximal consecutive ascending/descending run of at least four, once. Sequences do not wrap 9 to 0. Full House requires at least three of one digit and at least two of another.
- Nerd recognizes `π`, `e`, and `√2` reel symbols. The sheets do not define an item that creates irrational slots, so none has been invented; ordinary gameplay does not currently generate them. Bomb, W, blank, and Superposition are not irrational numbers.
- Cross gives six-slots one tenth their normal draw weight. Each Mining and Magnifier multiplies Rare/Legendary shop weights by 1.25/1.5. Each Graduate multiplies those weights by another 1.5 for new tools. Copies of Cross multiply the six-slot weight by another 0.1. These are relative weights, not percentage-point guarantees. Discounted prices round up; Bank interest rounds down; Wheel reduces reroll cost by $1, with a zero floor.
- Knife counts seen nines in groups of nine, carrying the remainder, reserving a legendary for each earned future shop, and paying $99 per completed group. Crystal Ball counts seen eights in groups of eight and carries the remainder. Rewards and replacements avoid already-owned IDs. An exhausted eligible reward pool grants Bug, even if Bug is already owned. Bug is excluded from starting inventories, ordinary random selections, and every shop offer (including Printer). Exhausted ordinary shop slots remain empty. New rewards do not recursively trigger from the same sighting that granted them.
- Package acts on acquisition and via Camera. It upgrades other trinkets by one rarity and caps Legendary at Legendary. It retains itself; replacements reset metadata. If no unowned replacement of the required rarity exists, the replacement is Bug. Butterfly likewise resets metadata and keeps rarity. Shops and rewards remain seeded and reproducible.

Balance defaults live in the engine and can be adjusted without changing the sheet definitions.

## Additional catalog defaults

- Increment All and Decrement All modify each displayed ordinary digit, wrapping at 0/9. They leave special symbols and bomb countdowns alone and count as one tool use. Modified digits count as sightings.
- Clover multiplies seven-slot draw weight by 2 per copy. Mechanical Arm multiplies Rare/Legendary tool-upgrade weights by 4 per copy, including Construction Worker rewards. Shop upgrades also benefit from Mining and Magnifier.
- Apple counts every unordered pair summing to 10. Yin & Yang compares the counts of odd and even digits, requiring at least one digit. Devil awards 666 once when at least one adjacent 666 occurs.
- Robot starts at 5 (binary 101), grows once per sighting of 0 or 1, and scores its current power. Octopus pays $8 per sighting of 8; Eight Ball doubles that payment. Settlement does not recount sightings.
- New Moon multiplies by 10 for each grey, non-digit display (including blanks, W, and unchosen Superposition). Bomb countdowns are digits. Checkered Flag tops up the score after all multipliers and before goal rewards or Snail checks. No Tools checks successful tool uses during the round, including Camera.
- Each Bicycle reduces shop rerolls by $2; Wheel reduces them by $1 per copy, with a zero floor. Each Hammer/Backpack adds one upgrade/trinket offer to shops and rerolls. Each Printer adds an offer copying a random owned non-Bug trinket at twice its normal price.
- Replication Experiment and Novel Experiment grant their reward on acquisition, or when explicitly activated by Camera, not each spin. Replication copies another owned instance uniformly, preserving independent metadata; an empty pool yields Bug. Novel Experiment uses the normal unowned random reward pool. Copies (including Printer purchases) preserve metadata without re-running acquisition hooks, preventing recursive copying. Starting selections exclude both experiments to retain two distinct starting trinkets.
