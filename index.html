How to play

You have 20 spins, which spin 6 reels to land on random digits.

Use tools to change what digits get scored.

Depending on what you get, your trinkets turn those digits into points!

Beat that spin's goal for a cash bonus, then buy trinkets, tools, or tool upgrades in the shop to get you even more points. Missing a goal never ends your run!

(Hover or focus any item to learn what it does; tap trinkets to inspect them.)

(Also hover or tap a reel's magnifying glass to see its pool.)

Daily runs reset at midnight UTC. The same seed and choices produce the same results. Your run saves automatically in this browser.
