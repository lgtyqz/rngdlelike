A little luck. A little strategy.

You have 20 spins. Each reel lands on a digit from 0 to 9. Your trinkets turn those digits into points.

After spinning, select a tool, then a reel to change it. Tools recharge every spin. Hover or focus any item to learn what it does; tap trinkets to inspect them.

Get points to activate your trinkets. Beat that spin’s goal for a cash bonus, then buy trinkets, tools, or upgrades in the shop. Missing a goal never ends your run.

Daily runs reset at midnight UTC. The same seed and choices produce the same results. Your run saves automatically in this browser.
